# BR-002 — AI-assisted Silver transformation, constrained to minimal metadata

## Status

Under review

## Ask as stated

"Process bronze data using AI in silver with giving ai minimal metadata."

## Interpretation

The ask is still ambiguous. The recommended interpretation is:

- AI drafts metadata-derived transformation or rule specs.
- Silver's existing rule engine executes them deterministically.
- A human reviews or promotes the draft before it takes effect.

This is the only reading consistent with the phrase "minimal metadata" as written.

## Not recommended

AI as the live, per-row, in-write-path transformation engine for Silver is not the recommended interpretation.

That would be a different and larger decision because it would place AI into the actual transformation path, which is beyond the current Bronze exception and requires a Silver-specific decision record.

## Minimal metadata boundary

The metadata allowed for this use should be schema and profile facts only, such as:

- column names, types, and nullability
- drift/fingerprint history
- nesting/structure shape
- aggregate non-identifying statistics
- existing AI metadata drafts
- run-level audit facts

The metadata must not contain:

- actual row values
- row samples
- business-column values used to infer corrected outputs
- identifying aggregates on columns flagged as sensitive

## Required decisions before approval

1. Confirm the reading: advisory rule drafting vs. live Silver transformation.
2. Decide if the metadata payload may include any aggregate statistics at all.
3. Confirm sequencing: does this attach to the Silver epic now, or wait until Silver core work lands?
4. Name the business owner and urgency.

## Open questions

- Is the intended mode advisory or autonomous?
- Does "minimal metadata" allow any value-level statistics, or only schema and drift facts?
- Does this become part of #205 now or after Silver core work is delivered?
- Who owns the business outcome and what is the quantified impact?
- What provider or cost model should this use?

## Approval gate

This BR should stay in Under review until:

- the intended AI boundary is explicit,
- the Silver sequencing is confirmed,
- the business owner and impact are named,
- and the final decision is recorded as advisory rule drafting or a Silver-scoped write-path exception.

## Final read

BR-002 is a legitimate design question, but it is not yet a fully scoped issue. It should remain in review until the intended AI boundary and the Silver sequencing are explicit.
